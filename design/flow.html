D deterministic · G generative · H human. Steps 2 and 3 never reach a screen: a candidate whose evidence does not resolve is counted as an error and discarded before anyone sees it. Step 7 rejections go to the run log, so a reviewer never meets an illegal transition. Only steps 4, 5 and 8 are visible, and all three land on the proposal card.
